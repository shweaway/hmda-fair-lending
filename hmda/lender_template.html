Click a column to sort; click a row to inspect. MM share = share of applications from majority-minority (50%+) tracts; Δ = lender minus market (pp). B–W gap = Black denial rate minus White denial rate (pp). Exempt % = records with EGRRCPA-exempt pricing. Excl. 4–8 = withdrawn, closed-incomplete, purchased, and preapproval records at this institution — not counted in Apps/Orig/Denial % (see scope note below).

Scope note: Apps, Orig, and Denial % on this page cover only consumer-purpose records where the institution reached a credit decision — HMDA action-taken codes 1 (loan originated), 2 (approved but not accepted), and 3 (application denied). Applications withdrawn by the applicant (4), files closed for incompleteness (5), loans the institution purchased rather than originated (6), and preapproval requests (7, 8) are excluded from those figures; they are counted separately in the "Excl. 4–8" column. Business/commercial-purpose records are excluded entirely. This mirrors the standard denial-rate methodology used in CFPB and academic HMDA research, but it means these totals will read lower than a raw count of every record an institution reported.
